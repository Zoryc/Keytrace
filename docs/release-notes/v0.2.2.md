## Keytrace 0.2.2

Faster conversions with exactly the same results.

### Faster
- **Best** (Ensemble): a 2-minute piece now takes about **13 seconds** instead of 21 on an RTX 5070 Ti.
- **Faster** (Transkun V2): about **4 seconds** instead of 9. It is now about three times as quick as Best.
- **Every note is identical.** The speed-up comes from how the work is handed to the graphics card, not from changing the maths: on 27 test recordings, every note, velocity, pedal and MIDI file came out bit-for-bit the same as in 0.2.0.
- **Quicker start:** the window opens before the transcription engine loads, and a short warm-up at launch makes the first conversion as fast as the next ones.

### Fixes
- After a rare numerical error in Aria-AMT, later conversions could keep failing until Keytrace was restarted. They now recover on their own.
- Files sent to Keytrace while it was still starting could be skipped.
- Cancelling a conversion while its audio was still loading now stops it straight away.
- The preview audio is written alongside the transcription, using less memory on long recordings.

### Install
1. Download **Keytrace-Setup-0.2.2.exe** below and run it. If Windows says *"Windows protected your PC"*, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**; the installer isn't code-signed.
2. On a new install, Setup downloads Python, PyTorch for your NVIDIA GPU and the models, about 3 GB.

To upgrade, install over your current version: your settings and conversion history are kept.

You need Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and about 8 GB of free space. An NVIDIA GPU is strongly recommended.
