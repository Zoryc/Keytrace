## Keytrace 0.2.0

Turn piano recordings into MIDI, accurately and privately on your own PC.

### Highlights
- **New name and look.** MIDI Converter is now **Keytrace**, with a calm, simple interface: drop a recording and click **Save MIDI**. It comes in light and dark versions, with a new logo.
- **Ensemble engine.** Transkun V2 transcribes, and Aria-AMT gives a second opinion where Transkun was unsure. It finds **98.6 % of the notes** on the MAESTRO test set and stays above 96 % on phone, MP3, noisy and reverberant recordings.
- **Three simple options:** Quality (Best / Faster), Pedal (keep / bake into notes / leave out) and Dynamics (as played / even). Everything else is under **More settings**. Pedal and dynamics changes update an open result instantly.
- **Right-click to convert.** Choose **Convert to MIDI with Keytrace** on any audio or video file in Explorer.
- **Piano only.** Keytrace focuses on solo piano.

### Install
1. Download **Keytrace-Setup-0.2.0.exe** below and run it. If Windows says *"Windows protected your PC"*, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**; the installer isn't code-signed.
2. Setup downloads Python, PyTorch for your NVIDIA GPU and the models, about 3 GB.

You need Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and about 8 GB of free space. An NVIDIA GPU is strongly recommended.

### Upgrading from MIDI Converter 0.1.0
Install over it. Your conversion history and settings move to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Keytrace` the first time Keytrace starts.
